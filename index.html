Here is a comprehensive guide to understanding this remarkable nut. 01 🌱 Origin & History Pecans are the only major tree nut native to North America. Indigenous peoples cultivated them for centuries before European settlers discovered their incredible nutritional value. The name "pecan" comes from the Algonquin word meaning "nut requiring a stone to crack." Georgia has been the leading pecan producing state for decades, with some orchards dating back over 100 years. 02 🌿 Growing Conditions Georgia's climate — with hot summers, mild winters, and adequate rainfall — creates the perfect environment for pecan cultivation. Pecan trees require long growing seasons of 180–200 frost-free days, well-drained soils, and full sun. Georgia's unique soil composition gives its pecans a distinctively rich, buttery flavor profile unmatched anywhere else in the world. 03 ⚙️ Harvesting Process Georgia pecans are harvested between October and December. Mechanical shakers vibrate the trees to release mature nuts onto the ground, which are then collected by harvesters. After harvesting, nuts are cleaned, dried to optimal moisture content (below 4%), sorted by size and quality, and shelled using precision equipment that preserves the halves intact. 04 🔬 Grading Standards All our pecans meet USDA Grade A standards — the highest classification. Grading considers size uniformity, moisture content, color, and freedom from defects. Mammoth halves (the largest grade) measure over 250 halves per pound. Our shelled pecan halves are carefully screened to remove any shell fragments, ensuring 100% edible product for your customers. 05 📦 Packaging & Storage Pecans are packed in food-grade, vacuum-sealed bags to preserve freshness and extend shelf life. Properly stored shelled pecans last 6 months at room temperature, 18 months refrigerated, and up to 2 years frozen. Our packaging meets US food safety standards and international import requirements, with all mandatory labeling included. 06 🌍 Global Market The global pecan market is projected to reach $3.64 billion by 2034, driven by rising health consciousness worldwide. Emerging economies across Asia, the Middle East, and beyond represent exciting growth markets for premium American pecans, with demand accelerating as middle classes expand and health awareness increases.
